PARADISEC states that its catalogue can also be searched freely through the OLAC route. It explains that when citing material, the "Cite as" format and DOI of the individual item should be used. However, searchability does not mean unrestricted use of every file. The public access conditions state that access presupposes the depositor's permission and that separate restrictions accompany each item. Even for the same recording, the speaker, the recorder, the classifier, the language community and the later listener each hold different knowledge and authority.

This is a summary of the public guidance and does not substitute for permission to use individual materials.
