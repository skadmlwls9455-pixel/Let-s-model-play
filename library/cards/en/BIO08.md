A 2018 plant study reported that glutamate acts as a wound signal. The research team explains that ion channels of the GLUTAMATE RECEPTOR-LIKE family convert this signal into a rise in intracellular calcium concentration, and that the change propagates to distant organs and induces defense responses. This result shakes the impression that plants are merely a passive background, but it does not mean that a pain experience like that of humans was directly measured. Signal, response, regulation, and experience are explanations at different levels.

The research team's report within the scope of the abstract was summarized. It is not turned into a study that determines whether plants have pain or consciousness.
