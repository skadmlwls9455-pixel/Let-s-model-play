The AI Song Contest study surveyed the co-creation experiences of 13 teams of musicians and developers, 61 people in total. The abstract reports approaches that combine the outputs of several small models, approaches that generate many samples and then select among them, and strategies that adjust the direction of generation or rank the results.

Because this is a case study of one particular contest, it does not represent the behavior of all musicians or all generation tools. This material did not actually generate music files or evaluate them by listening.
