GW150914, observed by LIGO on September 14, 2015, is a gravitational-wave event from the merger of two black holes. The official explanation calls the part in which the frequency of the wave increases as the two bodies come closer a chirp. However, the sound heard here did not cross space as vibrations of air. GWOSC explains that gravitational waves are not sound themselves, and that the released audio files may have had whitening and band-pass processing to suppress noise, or a frequency shift, applied.

The description of the observation network at that time on the official page is not read as the current operating status of the observatories.
