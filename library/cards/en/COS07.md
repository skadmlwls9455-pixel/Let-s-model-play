The abstract of the 1984 paper by Shechtman et al. reports that long-range orientational order was observed in an aluminum-manganese metallic phase, and describes its icosahedral symmetry as incompatible with lattice translation. The same abstract reports that the diffraction spots were sharp like those of a crystal but could not be indexed to any Bravais lattice. It is a scene in which the familiar repeating lattice and the observed order do not fit at the same time. The explanation differs depending on whether regular is taken to mean the existence of a repeating unit or that orientational relationships are maintained over long distances.

The scope of verification is the experimental report in the abstract of the original paper. The diffraction data of the original experiment were not reanalyzed, and the full mathematical definition of quasicrystals was not verified.
