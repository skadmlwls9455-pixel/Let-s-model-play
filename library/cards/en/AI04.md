When a model is asked what it was thinking, an explanation may come back. However, the plausibility of that explanation alone does not justify judging that an actual internal state was read. Anthropic's 2025 research summary describes an experiment in which a concept is injected into an internal representation and the model's self-report is then checked against it. It reported responses that noticed the injected concept under some conditions, but wrote that even with the best protocol, the rate at which Opus 4.1 showed that type of awareness was about 20%. The authors study functional access and the ability to report, and stated explicitly that the results do not tell whether Claude is conscious.

The roughly 20% is the result of a specific experiment and protocol, and is neither general conversational accuracy nor a probability of consciousness.
