That the six arms of a snow crystal look alike does not mean a channel for passing a blueprint among the arms is needed. Libbrecht's explanation as a researcher says that the arms grow independently and undergo similar changes in temperature and humidity together, so their shapes can come to resemble each other. The morphology diagram also summarizes tendencies according to growth conditions. When humidity is high and growth is fast, complex branches tend to appear, and at low humidity and slow growth, simple forms tend to appear.

Not all natural snow crystals are perfectly symmetrical. The description of remaining unsolved problems on the researcher's site is not extended into a complete verdict on the whole of recent research.
