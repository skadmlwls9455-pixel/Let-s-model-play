Computational origami research presents a constructive result that a scaled silhouette of any connected polygonal region, including cases with holes, can be folded from a single rectangular sheet of paper. The same research builds connected two-colored polygonal patterns when the two sides of the paper differ in color, and also addresses the problem of wrapping a polyhedron with a strip of paper.

The subjects of the theorem are the stated mathematical origami model and connected polygonal and polyhedral shapes. It is not a claim that any shape can be folded easily from arbitrary physical materials and thicknesses.
