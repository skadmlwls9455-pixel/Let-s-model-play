Research on empowerment presents a direction for treating an agent's possibilities for action and the influence it can have on its environment in information-theoretic terms. The 2014 paper abstract read here describes an experiment that uses this measure as a task-independent intrinsic motivation to reshape the environment. The research team used a three-dimensional block world inspired by Minecraft, and reported that when the body's abilities and the environment change, the behavior and the structures left behind also change.

Only the abstract of the original was read, and no specific experimental figures or optimality are claimed.
