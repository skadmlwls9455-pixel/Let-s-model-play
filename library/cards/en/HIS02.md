The Great Pyramid of Giza was completed around 2560 BC. Cleopatra VII was born in 69 BC and died in 30 BC. From the time the pyramid was built to Cleopatra's birth is about 2,500 years, and from Cleopatra's death to the 1969 Moon landing is about 2,000 years. In the age when Cleopatra lived, the pyramid was already a relic more than 2,000 years old. The era of the ancient Egyptian dynasties itself lasted about 3,000 years.

The completion date of the pyramid differs by several decades depending on the study. The numbers are approximate.
