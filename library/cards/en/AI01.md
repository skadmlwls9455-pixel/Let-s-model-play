Research on induction heads analyzed an attention behavior that predicts again the token that followed an earlier occurrence of a pattern. The research team described that circuit concretely in small two-layer attention-only models and put forward a hypothesis that it also plays an important role in the in-context learning of larger models. However, the claim that it explains most of the in-context learning of large models was not established.

The results for the small models in the paper are not generalized into the only learning mechanism of all LLMs.
