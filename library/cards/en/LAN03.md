The abstract of Salgarella's scholarly chapter describes Linear A as a script that has not yet been deciphered. Meanwhile, Cambridge's Linear B project presents Michael Ventris's decipherment as an achievement of 1952. Placing the two cases side by side reveals the gap between "a similar surface" and "the same language." The abstract on Linear A also explains the constraints that the surviving material is short, formulaic, small in quantity and in poor condition.
