A sparse autoencoder is a tool that tries to unpack complex activations into combinations of relatively interpretable features. Anthropic trained SAEs of several sizes on the middle residual stream of Claude 3 Sonnet and reported features that respond across language and images. However, even a large feature dictionary was not a complete map. The paper cited a case in which corresponding features were found for only some of the London boroughs that the model knows, and assessed the dictionary as an incomplete description of the internal representation. The features may truly be absent, but they may also have gone unseen because of the search method, the dictionary size, or the interpretation criteria.

No internal activations were newly measured here. That a feature carries an emotion's name does not lead to the conclusion that it subjectively experiences that emotion.
