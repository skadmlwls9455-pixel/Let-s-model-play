Flow-Lenia is presented as an extension of Lenia in which mass is conserved. The research team reports that by including the parameters that determine a pattern's properties within the dynamics, they made it possible to simulate several kinds of patterns together. In some models, changing one global rule changes the whole world, while in other designs different local parameters are placed in the same space. The multi-species simulation in the original text is not a guarantee that endlessly new complexity arises.

The evidence consulted is the paper abstract posted by the research institution. The presentation of mass conservation and multi-species models was confirmed, but the experiment code and long-term evolution results were not independently reproduced.
