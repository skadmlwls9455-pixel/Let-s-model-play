POET is an algorithm that binds together the process of generating environmental challenges and the process of optimizing the agents that solve them. The original paper's abstract explains that it allows better solutions to be transferred between different problems. The phrase "open-ended" in the paper's title is not read as empirical demonstration of unlimited progress.

POET's design and research results concern limited experimental environments.
