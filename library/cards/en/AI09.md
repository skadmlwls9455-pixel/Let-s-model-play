World Models is research that learns a policy by using a model that compresses observations of an environment and predicts how they change over time. The authors reported an experiment in which a policy trained in a generated internal environment was transferred to the original reinforcement learning environment. Here the expression dream refers to the generated simulation, and it is not an expression that proves a subjective dream experience. The internal environment may have gaps. If the agent exploits those gaps, it may perform well inside but fail in the original environment. The authors also described an approach that reduces such exploitation by adjusting the uncertainty of the generated environment.

The paper's actual environment means the original game environment. The success of that transfer is not extended into generalization to the real physical world at large.
