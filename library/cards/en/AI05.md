Showing a model one new sentence is different from modifying the model's parameters. The ROME project changed a factual association in a particular GPT-family model through a small rank-one edit to one MLP module, and examined whether the change also appeared when the question was asked in other wording. The follow-up MEMIT extended this toward editing thousands of factual associations at once, and evaluated not only edit success but also generalization and the preservation of unrelated knowledge. Here the word memory refers to a functional representation of the factual associations the research deals with. It does not mean that a person's whole life or every reasoning ability was edited.

The results of the models and fact edits evaluated in the papers are not generalized to arbitrary recent models or to all knowledge.
