The Webb telescope observes infrared light, which is invisible to the human eye. The NASA and STScI explanation of image processing states that the individual black-and-white exposure data are organized, assigned colors, and combined, and that in general blue, green, and red are matched in that order to wavelengths from short to long. The first deep-field image of SMACS 0723 also has an actual correspondence table. F444W was assigned to red, F356W to orange, F200W and F277W to green, and F090W and F150W to blue. Therefore, it is not appropriate either to conclude from the mere fact that colors were assigned that the data were fabricated, or to believe that this is how the scene would look to the naked eye.

The specific filter-to-color correspondence on the official page is not applied uniformly to all Webb images.
