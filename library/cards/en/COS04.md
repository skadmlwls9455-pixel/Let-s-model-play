Planck's visualization of the polarization of the cosmic microwave background is not simply a photograph of the color of the universe. In the official explanation, the color scale represents temperature differences in the cosmic microwave background, and the texture that looks like surface grain represents the direction of polarization. The same page states that the data were filtered to center on a particular angular scale in order to present them in an easy-to-view form, and explains that variations on smaller scales were also actually observed. Looking at the flat or smooth parts of the map and concluding that there is no structure there overlooks the processing.

The filtering information in the official figure description concerns that visualization. It is not generalized to say that all CMB analysis data went through the same filter.
