A research team that analyzed Cassini data identified phosphates, which contain phosphorus, in salt-rich ice grains ejected by Enceladus. NASA's 2023 announcement explains that this result came from the analysis of archived observational data. However, the same announcement clearly distinguishes that finding a needed ingredient alone does not establish the existence of life.

The statement at that time that life has not been found is not presented as the result of an independent, up-to-date exhaustive survey.
