In the WASP-39b study, an absorption feature at about 4.05 micrometers was linked to sulfur dioxide. NASA's introductory material and the original paper by Tsai et al. explain the identification of this substance. An important layer the original paper adds is the model comparison concerning the cause. The researchers calculated the photochemistry with ARGO, ATMO, KINETICS, and VULCAN and reported results that explain the observed feature. However, these used common conditions, such as cloud-free one-dimensional calculations. That several codes produce similar results is different from saying that every assumption has been independently verified.

Evidence of photochemistry is not evidence of the existence of life.
