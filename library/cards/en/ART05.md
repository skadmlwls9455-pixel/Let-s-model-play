An L-system is a grammatical system that rewrites the symbols of a string in parallel. The authors' research introduction states that attaching a way of interpreting symbols as drawings makes it possible to express change over time and plant models. The original text addresses the problem of inferring rules from observed strings.

The paper deals with rule inference under specific conditions, and it does not mean that the actual growth laws can be recovered from every plant photograph.
