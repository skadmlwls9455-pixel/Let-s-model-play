On the morning of June 30, 1908, a great explosion occurred in the sky near the Podkamennaya Tunguska River in Siberia. About 80 million trees in about 2,000 km² of forest fell facing outward from the center of the explosion. The explosion energy is estimated at from several megatons to over ten megatons of TNT. No impact crater was found, and it is explained as an asteroid or comet fragment tens of meters across that exploded in the air at a height of 5 to 10 km before reaching the ground. The first scientific investigation came 19 years later, in 1927, led by Leonid Kulik. It is counted as the largest impact event in recorded history.

Eyewitness accounts and seismograph and barometer records remain, but the range of estimates is still wide as to whether the object was an asteroid or a comet and how large it was.
