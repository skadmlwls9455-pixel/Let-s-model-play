Planarian regeneration research explores whether bioelectric states take part in controlling morphogenesis. A 2017 paper, after temporarily perturbing the bioelectric network, reported a latent state in which the subsequent regeneration pattern differs even in individuals that look normal. The research team explains that manipulation that reverses the electrical state can restore regeneration of the normal form. Here the expression "shape memory" is a functional interpretation of a physical state that maintains a target form. It does not mean that the animal consciously recalls the shape of its body.

The researchers' interpretation of bioelectric control is distinguished from a universal law established independently. This material is not a guide for carrying out animal amputation or drug treatment and does not propose further biological experiments.
