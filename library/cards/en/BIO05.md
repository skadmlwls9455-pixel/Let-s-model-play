To examine relational learning, re-matching a familiar picture is not enough. The review by the researcher Giurfa surveys experiments in which bees were made to choose a stimulus that was the same as, or different from, a sample at the entrance. Bees trained with colors transferred the correspondence relation to patterns they saw for the first time, and transfer in the opposite direction was also reported. The review also introduces transfer between odors and colors. However, "concept" has an operational meaning as used in these experiments, and it is not established to be identical to the experience of concepts that humans explain in language. For this material, a follow-up review by the original author was read in place of the full text of the original papers.

The review is not counted as an independent replication of the original experiments. The transfer in one relational task is not extended to all abstract reasoning abilities.
