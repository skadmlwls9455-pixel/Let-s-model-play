ChainQueen is presented as a differentiable physical simulator that handles deformable objects including contact. The authors report that it can be combined with inference, control, and co-design problems and that they used it on several soft-robot control tasks. Improvement within a simulation cannot be directly carried over into performance of a physical robot.

The evidence consulted is the paper abstract on the authors' site. The simulator's accuracy and its performance when transferred to reality were not independently measured.
