Imagining an octopus arm as one long wire loses an important structure. A 2025 study examined the axial nerve cord of the arm along its length and reported that nerve cells are arranged in a segmented pattern. The nerves leading to the suckers exit between those segments, creating a spatial correspondence with each sucker. The research institution's description likewise states that nerves from several segments connect to different regions of the muscle.

This anatomical study does not prove that each arm has an independent consciousness. The discovery of a segmented structure is also different from a complete account of the actual control algorithm.
