The Whitney Museum of American Art describes Harold Cohen's AARON as a program that manipulates symbols. According to the exhibition text, AARON combines formal rules with random events, evaluates the progress of a composition through internal feedback, and uses knowledge about subjects and drawing strategies that the artist put into the code.

The exhibition text is a primary institutional source introducing AARON's historical construction and works. It is not material that establishes any inner experience of the program or the detailed implementation of every version.
