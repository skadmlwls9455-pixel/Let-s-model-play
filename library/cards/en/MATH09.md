Comparing photographs of tangled string by eye and comparing the mathematical features of a knot are different tasks. The official data description of KnotInfo states that it stores a value and, where available, a reference link paired with each invariant. On the actual lookup screen, a knot can be selected by crossing number, and several polynomial invariants such as the Alexander and Jones polynomials can be chosen together with other properties. The number of noticeable bends, the crossings that appear in a drawing, the value of a chosen invariant, and the name in the database are information at different levels. However, the fact that the database was consulted does not mean that every entry was computed independently.

The functions and provenance structure of the database were confirmed, and the invariant values of particular knots were not computed directly here. The equality of one invariant alone is not taken to establish the equivalence of knots.
