# Pixel-look references — open-source 3D pixel-art renderers (three.js / WebGL)

Surveyed 2026-11-04. "Verified" = README and/or shader source fetched from raw.githubusercontent.com and read.
Live demos on github.io / *.dev / vercel.app / itch.io / discourse.threejs.org were **blocked by the proxy** — looks are
inferred from source, screenshots in READMEs and third-party descriptions. Companion to `three-pixel-look.md` (our r128 recipe).

## Verified entries (ranked by usefulness to the attic)

1. **three.js official `RenderPixelatedPass` + example `webgl_postprocessing_pixel`** — https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/jsm/postprocessing/RenderPixelatedPass.js · https://threejs.org/examples/webgl_postprocessing_pixel.html
   Look: crisp iso crates/crystal, 1-px dark depth outlines + light normal highlights on top faces, hard shadow map, emissive crystal.
   Params: `pixelSize` 6 (GUI 1–16); beauty + normal targets at `screen/pixelSize`, HalfFloat, NearestFilter; **outline = depth** `diff=Σclamp(dN-d,0,1)` over 4 taps → `floor(smoothstep(0.01,0.02,diff)*2)/2` (0/.5/1 levels), **highlight = normal** `(1-dot(n,nN))*depthIndicator*smoothstep(-.01,.01,dot(n-nN,vec3(1)))`, `step(0.1, Σ)` → colour × `(1 - 0.4*dei)` or `× (1 + 0.3*nei)` (`depthEdgeStrength .4`, `normalEdgeStrength .3`). Lighting: Ambient `0x757f8e`×3, Directional `0xfffecd`×1.5 shadow 2048, Spot `0xffc100` 10 / angle π/16. Camera: Ortho ±aspect×±1, `y = 2·tan(30°)`, z 2 (≈30° elevation). Motion: `pixelAlignedPanning` (frustum snapped to the low-res pixel grid), `pixelAlignedObjects` (object pos snapped to pixel-world size), `rotationSnap 15°`, `cameraRotationSnap 9°`, `cameraZoomSnap 0.1`. Emissive pulsing `sin(t*3)*.5+.5`.
2. **KodyJKing/hello-threejs** (449★, origin of #1; video https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jFevm02NJ5M) — https://github.com/KodyJKing/hello-threejs
   Same shader as #1 but: render res = `screen/6`; **UnrealBloomPass(.4 strength, .1 radius, .9 threshold) at full screen res *between* pixel pass and a second nearest-neighbour `PixelatePass`** so glow is re-quantised to the pixel grid; Ambient `0x2d3645`×1.5 (cool, dark), Sun `0xfffc9c`×.5, Spot `0xff8800`; all textures `NearestFilter`.
3. **sloemo01/diorama** (327★, three r170 "playbook + code kit", MIT) — https://github.com/sloemo01/diorama (`templates/post.js`, `SKILL.md` "Palette And The Post Pass", "Numbers That Make It Read")
   Look: printed-toy dioramas on paper white — flat palette fills, ink contour, stipple only on ramps; the most "premium" of the set.
   Params: post order **composite→gamma(1/2.2)→grade (sat 1.07, contrast 1.028)→ink→gated 8×8 Bayer→64-colour palette snap (nearest, channel weights 1/1.12/0.93, near-tie <0.0005 → lighter entry)→rim dither**. Ink: neighbour-luma edge `pow(edge,0.4545)`, `smoothstep(0.13,0.34)`, mix 0.62 toward warm near-black `(0.085,0.082,0.076)`, suppressed on silhouette rim. Dither amplitude tiny: `d*(0.0012+0.019*gate)` with `gate=smoothstep(0.004,0.055,edge)` — flat surfaces stay flat. Buffer = 1 target px per CSS px (chunkiness comes from palette+ink, not low res), `renderScale` 1.2–1.5 for small subjects. Camera: **perspective fov 20.5, azimuth 45°, elevation 33°**, distance solved by bisection to fill 0.84/0.86, no orbit. Light: Sun `0xfff8ee`×3.3 shadow 2048 ±13 bias −0.00022 normalBias .025 radius 1; Hemi `0xd6ecf6/0x6f6a60`×0.24; fill `0xa9cbe2`×0.46; env 0.18. Night: key `0x93a7c4` 0.45–0.65, hemi `0x1b2740/0x090b12` 0.16, practical `0xffb066` 1.0–1.4 (**one real point light at the hero lamp**, rest emissive + floor-pool decal), lift black to `0x0b1020`, "keep three dark values", contact-shadow patch under every prop.
4. **niccolofanton/dithering-shader** (294★, R3F + pmndrs/postprocessing, MIT) — https://github.com/niccolofanton/dithering-shader (`src/dithering-shader/DitheringShader.ts`)
   Look: halftone/retro helmet, envmap lit. Params: `pixelSize = gridSize*pixelSizeRatio`, pixelated UV `floor(fragCoord/pixelSize)*pixelSize/res`, 4×4 Bayer threshold on luminance (knock-out 1-bit or colour mode), **bloom passes both before and after the dither** (pre-bloom feeds the dither, post-bloom softens). Pattern from Shadertoy Klems ltSSzW.
5. **WoodNeck/three-pixelate-showcase** (7★, MIT) — https://github.com/WoodNeck/three-pixelate-showcase (`src/layer/PixelatedLayer.ts`, `src/pass/OutlinePass.ts`)
   Look: hand-drawn pigeon on stone, Lospec palette (Ice-Cream-GB/Sweetie-16/Endesga-16). Params: **`pixelPerUnit = 8`**, RT = `drawingBuffer/8`, ortho camera with `zoom = pixelPerUnit` so 1 world unit = 8 art px; camera `rotateZ(-45°)·rotateX(60°)`; `DepthTexture(UnsignedShortType)`, 1-px depth outline drawn after pixelation; palette as 1-D texture LUT; sharp UI in a separate full-res foreground layer.
6. **codedgar/three-psx** (1★, MIT, PS1 toolkit) — https://github.com/codedgar/three-psx
   Look: night bus stop / golden-hour pool / neon rain rooftop — strong mood from fog + one warm point light. Params: 320×240 target, nearest upscale `integer` (pixel-perfect) or `fit`; Bayer 2/4/8 or IGN noise; **15-bit colour quantisation after bloom/grade**; chunky low-res bloom 0.25; vertex snap intensity (dial down on coarse geometry: "silhouettes crawl"); one directional + ambient + one per-vertex point light quadratic falloff; textures `NoColorSpace` (gamma-space lighting on purpose); fog `setFog(0x101018, 4, 30)`.
7. **AdityaKodez/adityaojha `dithered-isometric-scenes/assets/diorama-runtime.js`** (single-file, agent-generated style) — https://github.com/AdityaKodez/adityaojha
   Params: `pixelSize 2` (clamp 1–4), ortho ±8 from `(12,10,12)`, frustum fitted to scene bounds, `renderer.setPixelRatio(1)` + CSS `image-rendering: pixelated`; shader: premultiplied → sRGB → **`floor(c*levels + 0.5 + ((bayer4+0.5)/16 − 0.5)*dither)/levels` with `levels 8`, `dither 0.85`**; pure-white preserved; Hemi `#f8f1df/#8b9992`×1.8, key `#fff4df`×2.5 shadow 1024 bias −0.0003 normalBias .025, PCFShadowMap.
8. **DavJCosby/Palette-Matcher** (C++/OpenGL, not three — technique reference) — https://github.com/DavJCosby/Palette-Matcher
   Gooch warm/cool shading interpolated **in OKLab** (`K_COOL (64,6,191)/255`, `K_WARM (255,223,97)/255`, α .6, β .2, Fresnel .3) + shadow map, low-res lighting pass → depth 1-px edges + OKLab nearest-palette with 4×4 Bayer → upscale. 16-colour Lospec palettes.
9. **hwanhuh/three.js-pixel-art-shader** (1★, MIT, demo on gh-pages) — https://github.com/hwanhuh/three.js-pixel-art-shader
   Cel/quantised `PixelArtMaterial` (`ditherStrength 0.3`), inverted-hull `OutlineMaterial` (`thickness 0.02`, scale 1.02, colour `#111`), `PixelationPass` 320×240 + CRT curvature .3 / scanlines .1 (skip CRT for cozy).
10. **bandinopla PS1 ShaderMaterial gist** (Roman Liutikov article) — https://gist.github.com/bandinopla/497bcd86ba2bb662e0ab4cb47190748e
    Vertex snap `floor(res*pos.xy)/res` at 320×240; YUV quantise `uColorDepth 16`; 8×8 ordered dither blending floor/ceil levels, threshold `(d+1)/64`; affine UVs; per-vertex Lambert.

## What makes it look premium (ranked)

1. **Two edge signals, opposite signs**: depth edge *darkens* (×0.6, 2-level `floor(smoothstep(.01,.02)*2)/2`), normal edge *lightens* (×1.3) only on the nearer face → every top face gets a 1-px light rim, every silhouette a 1-px dark line (#1, #2).
2. **Palette snap + ink, not low res, carries the "pixel art" read**: diorama renders at 1 px/CSS px and still looks hand-drawn because of 64 colours + 0.62 ink; use p=2–3 for us, but never rely on p alone (#3).
3. **Dither is gated to tonal ramps**: amplitude ≈0.001 flat / 0.02 on gradients (#3); ungated dither = "TV static" (its own tuning table). Our 4×4 Bayer amplitude should be ≤ 1 palette step and ideally edge-gated.
4. **Pixel-snapped camera & objects**: ortho frustum aligned to the low-res grid, object positions snapped to `pixelWorldSize`, rotation snapped 15°/9°, zoom 0.1 — kills shimmer during the room tilt (#1).
5. **One strong warm key + cool ambient, high ratio**: key 1.5–3.3 vs ambient/hemi 0.2–0.5, cool-tinted (`0x757f8e`, `0xd6ecf6`), shadow map 2048 with bias −0.0002 / normalBias .025, radius ≤1 (hard) (#1, #3, #7).
6. **Low-res bloom re-pixelated**: bloom (.4/.1/.9) at full res, then nearest-neighbour re-sample to the pixel grid (#2); or bloom before the dither so the glow gets dithered (#4, #6). Glow must be ≥2 px wide and a palette colour or it vanishes after the snap (#3).
7. **Night is a new ratio, not day dimmed**: moon `0x93a7c4` 0.5, hemi `0x1b2740/0x090b12` 0.16, lamp `0xffb066` 1.2 as the single real point light, black lifted to `0x0b1020`, three distinct dark values (#3).
8. **Locked, long-lens three-quarter view**: ortho or fov ≈20, azimuth 45°, elevation 30–33°; fit by solving distance to bounds, not by scaling geometry (#1, #3, #5).
9. **Fixed world→pixel ratio**: 8 art px per world unit (#5); props designed so no detail is < 1.5 px (speckle test: set dither 0, if noise stays it is sub-pixel geometry) (#3).
10. **Contact shadows under every prop** (soft radial decal) plus the shadow map; and textures `NearestFilter` with tiny texel counts (#2, #3).

## Apply to our attic (r128, no build)

Keep our pass order (linear→sRGB→grade→ink→Bayer→OKLab snap) but: (a) add the normal-edge *lighten* (×1.3, `step(0.1)`) from `RenderPixelatedPass` via a `MeshNormalMaterial` override target, and make the depth edge 2-level ×0.6 instead of a single threshold; (b) gate the 4×4 Bayer by local luma edge (`smoothstep(0.004,0.055)`) with amplitude ≈ ½ palette step, and break palette near-ties toward the lighter entry; (c) cut the 90-colour palette's duplicates so flat planks resolve to *one* entry (64 is plenty — diorama uses 64, Lospec sets 16); (d) set the scale rule **1 world unit = 8 art px, p = 2 at ≥720 px tall / p = 3 on phones**, so a 2.4 m attic ≈ 19 art px tall per metre → 1 art px ≈ 5 cm at p=3, design no prop detail under 2 art px (10 cm) and snap prop positions to `pixelWorldSize`; (e) ortho camera elevation 30–33°, azimuth 45°, frustum snapped to the grid, tilt animation snapped to 9° steps or damped so it moves in whole pixels; (f) lighting: one warm window key `0xfffecd`×1.5–2.5 shadow 2048 radius 1 bias −0.0002 normalBias .025, cool hemi `0xd6ecf6/0x6f6a60`×0.3, no other real lights by day; at night a single `0xffb066` PointLight at the lantern (distance 6, decay 2) + emissive lantern lens + floor pool decal, hemi `0x1b2740/0x090b12`×0.16, black lift `0x0b1020`; (g) rain/floodlight glow: bloom at low res (strength .4 radius .1 threshold .9) *before* the snap so it is palette-quantised, glows ≥2 px; (h) keep `NearestFilter` on all textures, add a soft contact-shadow disc under furniture, slight vignette is fine (cozy), **no film grain, no CRT** — every reference that reads "premium" (#1–#3) has calm flat fields and ink, not noise.
