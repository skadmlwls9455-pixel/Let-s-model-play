**Two deviations from the spec numbers, for crispness (hard line 11). Flag both for the owner:**
1. **Stickers at DPR 2.625 stay 61 CSS, not 85.** The bigger size needs a layer zoom of 4/3. Chrome holds zoom as a short float, so a zoom of 4/3 drifts the picture by a device px every few sticker px: only 75 % of the pixel blocks were exact, and 6/5.25 gave 99 %. The rule now grows n only when the zoom is a whole number of 1/64. That gives 96 CSS at 360×740 @2 and @3, 412×915 @2 and 390×844 @3.
2. **The '+' gap is 3 px at rest and 5 px on the pop, not 2 and 4.** The 59-px jeon is odd, so it cannot split evenly around an even gap. The centre column and row of each axis-aligned cut are left out as the knife's kerf, so the two sides are exact mirror images and the gap is 2·sep + 1.

## Decisions
- Kerf instead of a 2-px gap, as explained in the conclusion.
- The sticker zoom must be a whole number of 1/64, as explained in the conclusion.
- `pop` is passed as the brief says, including under reduced motion. It is a one-frame gap change, the same kind of motion as the existing kerf flash.
- On the cut frame the existing flash lights every opened gap (`R.gaps`), not only the new one. This is unchanged behaviour.
- The bell's ring marks show on odd frames 1, 3, 5 and 7, together with swing A.
- In `test-cook-live` L1, L3 and L6 the test now waits 1.2 s of real time before tapping '다시'. When CDP dispatches the last cut as a horizontal swipe within a few real ms, Chrome swallows the click of a tap that follows within about a second. I bisected it: it happens with the new cut order, a real wait fixes it, and a finger is never that fast. The '다시' tap is only accepted 0.9 s after the plate appears anyway.
- `test-cook` §15: the guide-length check had its line indices swapped. This is outside my named sections, but it is an orientation pin.
