## Decisions (small choices the brief left open)

1. **The cannot-load close picture.** The main game has no pixel close picture (its sheets use the text button '닫기'). I used the kitchen's own close icon, exported as `assets/icons/dish/close.png` (manifest key `icon.dish.close`) by the same icon tool, on the same paper chip as the kitchen's close.
2. **The loading octopus in the wait layer** is the boot strip (`assets/ui/loading_oct.png`, 8 frames of 56×39):
   - drawn at `round(2·dpr)` device px per art px, centred on whole device px;
   - the legs animate in place (steps(8));
   - still under reduced motion.

   I did not reuse `#boot` itself, because R is changing it this round.
3. **A frame closed before `ready`** (close button, Esc or the 6 s timeout) is set to `about:blank`. A late load therefore cannot open the kitchen in a hidden overlay and write an `si 0` pot. This is how "no pot is made by a kitchen that never loaded" holds even on a very slow network.
4. **Kkochi's guide offsets.** The slice guides still have K1's seeded offsets on this branch (K1 makes them 0). The kkochi art reads the real cut positions (`look.offs`), so the cubes stay continuous from the cut to the skewer either way. Once K1 is merged the cuts are exactly at x 64 / 80.
5. **Kkochi tap feedback.** `onEvent` is K1's, so "which cube was hit" is worked out in `paintDish` from the marks' counts (`lastHit`). That cube squashes on the hit frame, and on its third hit it drops in 2 frames.
6. **The tray's "back"** is the dialog's native `cancel` (Esc and Android back). A tap anywhere off the chips (including between them) closes it. A tap on the counter button's own spot while the tray is open does not close it (that spot means "cook"), so a double tap on the counter no longer blinks the tray shut (independent check; a time window did not work because headless Chromium delivered the second tap 0.5–1 s late).
7. **Straight-in opens pass the pot's recipe.** When a pot with progress waits, `openCook` passes that pot's recipe. The kitchen would resume it anyway (`si ≥ 1` wins), so this only makes `open` explicit.

## Left as is, and why

- **The tray covers the '산책 갈래?' main button.** At 360 and 412 width the bottom-centred tray overlaps it, and pieces of its text show beside the chips. This is the placement §3.5 asks for, so I kept it. Flagged for the owner/lead.
- **Kitchen still open behind a closed overlay.** If the overlay ever closes while the frame's kitchen stays open (a lost `close` message), `COOK.open` returns early on the next `open` and keeps the old dish. I saw this only under heavy CPU load in smoke runs while another browser suite ran at the same time; it never reproduced alone (4 targeted repros). Smoke now checks that the kitchen is closed before each tray-rule step. Not changed in code.
- **One flaky pre-existing Z check.** Z's '관성으로 흐르는 중에 재봉틀을 톡' (scene coasting, not my code) failed in two of six Z runs under load and passed in the others.
