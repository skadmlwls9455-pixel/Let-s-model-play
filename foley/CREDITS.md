All 27 sounds here (54 files, .ogg Opus + .m4a AAC) are edited from sounds published on OpenGameArt.org, nearly all of them real recordings (the source of the steam hiss, cook_steam, may be processed noise). We did not record them and nothing is AI-generated. Of the 28 source files, 26 are CC0 and 2 are CC BY. CC BY sources must be credited with the lines below (including the change note). CC0 sources need no credit; we list them as a courtesy.
